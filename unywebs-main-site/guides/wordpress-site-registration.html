Complete Guide to WordPress Site Registration with Unywebs

When publishing your WordPress website, you need three essential things: the build/design, a domain name (e.g. www.johnsmith.com), and hosting services.

Understanding Your Website Needs

Think of your website as your online storefront in the world of the internet. For your storefront, you need:

  • An address/location (your domain name)
  • Land to build it on (hosting services)
  • Someone or something to build the actual building (website design/development)

Unywebs Web Solutions gives you access to different tiers of these tools depending on your website preferences.

Getting Started: Step-by-Step Registration Process

Here's how to register the basic tools you'll need to get your WordPress website published with the assistance of the Unywebs Tech team.

Step 1: Create Your Unywebs Account

First, create a free Unywebs WS account here.

We recommend picking a secure but shareable password on an email that you can easily access for your business.

Important: When created, please save this information in your email for your reference. If you have been logged out, navigate back to the login page using the login link.

Step 2: Select Your Hosting Plan

Once logged in, follow these steps:

  1. Navigate to the menu and select Hosting > WordPress
  2. Select the hosting plan (in most cases, the basic plan works perfectly)
  3. Add your selection to your cart and select your terms

Step 3: Choose Your Domain

Next, enter your choice of domain here.

Note: If you already have a domain, skip this step. If you need to transfer your current domain to this account, you need to select the domain transfer item instead.

Select an available domain for your business. Remember, this is what people will type in to visit your brand's site.

Step 4: Complete Your Cart

  1. Add the domain item to your cart
  2. Continue to your cart
  3. Ensure both the domain and hosting plans are there

Important Reminder: Your website requires both items or it will not be active.

Additional Note: There are additional web tools you can browse on this site. However, all that is required to publish the site are the three items mentioned above.

If you need official company emails, click here.

Step 5: Checkout

Complete the checkout process using a credit card or PayPal account.

Step 6: Complete the Unywebs Completion Form

To ensure your website gets published correctly and on time, you must submit the setup form so the tech team can assist with linking your domain, installing WordPress, and activating your site.

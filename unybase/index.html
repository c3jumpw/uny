Your dashboard

See what you get.

Every subscription comes with a dashboard that keeps the credentials, domains and change requests behind your app in one place — checked every night, with a warning before anything expires.

Everything at a glance

Log in and see straight away what needs attention: credentials that failed their last check, the next domain due for renewal, and the change requests still waiting on your developer.

  • Problems are called out in orange, before they become outages
  • Your subscription status, always visible
  • Light and dark mode

Every key your app runs on, checked every night

Store the API keys, tokens and logins behind your website and apps. UnyBase tests each one against the real service and tells you the moment one stops working.

  • Encrypted before it's saved, hidden until you ask
  • Grouped by app or site, so nothing gets mixed up
  • Works with GitHub, Supabase, Vercel, ClickUp, systeme.io and any custom API

Safe to use, easy to hand over

Each credential carries what the next person needs: which account it belongs to, where it's used, when it expires, and a note on how to renew it.

  • Reveal auto-hides after 45 seconds, or copy without showing it
  • Expiry warnings 14, 7 and 1 days ahead
  • A history of every check, edit and reveal

Never lose a domain to a missed renewal

Keep every web address in one list: where it's registered, who manages its DNS, and when the domain and its SSL certificate renew. The ones due soonest sit at the top.

  • Warnings 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before renewal
  • Auto-renew status at a glance
  • Registrar and DNS logins noted for whoever needs them

Turn scattered notes into a clear to-do list

Jot down every edit you want on your site as you notice it. When you're ready, send them to your developer, a technician or an AI tool as one tidy, numbered list — and keep a dated record of what's been done.

  • Paste a list: one request per line
  • Pending, Sent, Done — always know what's waiting on whom
  • A dated changelog you can copy as release notes
